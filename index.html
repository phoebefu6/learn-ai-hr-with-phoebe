easy - everyone medium hands-on / deciding hardest
The three rails this whole course runs on: a person decides, and the record shows which person - with the reason written the same day. Rate the job, not the polish - undefined criteria quietly measure background. And people data is not prompt fodder - no CVs, health, grievances or named pay in a workspace. Kestrel is a fictional company and every number in it is invented. This is education for HR professionals, not legal advice; obligations vary by jurisdiction and the dates in this course have already moved once.
